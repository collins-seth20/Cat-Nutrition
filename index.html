EZComplete fur Cats premix

Manufacturer batch guide: for 2 lb (907 g) of boneless meat, use 53.3 g premix and 1½ cups water. Enter the raw boneless meat weight to calculate the powder amount. The premix catalog entry has no validated per-100-g nutrient profile, so nutrition review stays marked unknown. The manufacturer describes the finished diet as averaging 5% fat as-fed; fattier ground meats may not match that guidance. Use only as directed: boneless meat, no added organ or calcium, and do not add powder to an already complete diet. Food Fur Life recommends cooking poultry.
